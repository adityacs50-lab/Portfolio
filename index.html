Preface

On the maker of these plates

✦

Self-taught since fourteen. No bootcamp, no pedigree — a search bar, a compiler, and a refusal to accept "that's just how it works" as an answer. Everything in this codex was built the same way: strip the system to its physics, keep only what earns its place, ship it, and let reality grade the work. Six-plus production AI systems shipped solo, now compounding into deep-tech ventures — while reading for a B.Tech in AI & ML.

The plates that follow run from deterministic guardrails for AI agents and autonomy software for underground mines, to commissioned storefronts, to the foundational instruments where the method was forged. The line they trace points at AI product management — because past a certain point the hard problem isn't writing the code. It's deciding what deserves to exist.

Pl. ISRU-001

ISRU AI ↗

Autonomy software for GPS-denied extreme environments

Sensor-fusion SLAM autonomy for underground mining, where satellites can't see and the map goes stale with every blast cycle. ISRU fuses LiDAR, inertial, and odometry streams into a live model of the mine — so heavy machines navigate the dark with the confidence of surveyed ground. Built for places where positioning is not a service you subscribe to, but a problem you solve.

  • domain — slam · sensor fusion
  • sim success — 93.3%
  • collisions — 0% @ full visibility
  • 2025 —
  • yc f26 applicant
✦ GPS is a luxury. Physics is not.

Pl. STL-002

StateLock ↗

Deterministic guardrails for autonomous AI agents

A policy-enforcement gateway that intercepts an agent's actions before execution — permit, deny, or escalate, decided by deterministic rules instead of probabilistic LLM judgment. In live demo it halted a $500 unauthorized refund at 1.0 confidence; the production backend validates 7/7 PASS. Ships as a pip-installable Python SDK with a rule-approval workflow and developer portal, priced for fintech stakes.

  • stack — fastapi · next.js · supabase · railway
  • halted $500 rogue refund @ 1.0 confidence
  • 2026 —
  • founder · private beta
✦ A guardrail that's 99% reliable is a suggestion.

Pl. BTX-003

Batonyx

An autonomous agent for the Sales → Customer-Success handoff

B2B SaaS aimed at the exact moment revenue is most often fumbled: the handoff. When a deal closes, Batonyx reads the full record — Gong calls, Slack threads, Gmail — and delivers a Handoff Brief, an Expectation Ledger, and a Day-Zero Pre-Mortem before the kickoff call is even booked. The customer never has to repeat what they already told sales.

  • stack — next.js · groq
  • 2026
  • in active build
✦ Deals don't die at the close. They die in the handoff.
